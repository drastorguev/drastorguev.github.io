Cross-border Money Transfers - User Activity Forecasting

Project statement

A cross-border money transfer provider needs to forecast how many users will transact, and what that means for revenue and costs. Top-line forecasts do not show which customer groups or channels drive the change.

Requirements

  • Forecasts of total user activity over a planning horizon.
  • Links from activity to revenue and cost.
  • A breakdown into KPIs the business can act on.
  • Scenarios for planning and budgeting.

Approach

I would forecast activity by customer group and channel, then roll the results up to revenue and cost, so the drivers stay visible.

  1. Activity forecast: Transaction counts and values are forecast by customer group and corridor, with trend and seasonality modelled separately.
  2. Economics: Revenue and cost per transaction are applied to the activity forecast to produce financial outcomes.
  3. Scenarios: Base, upside and downside cases show the range of outcomes for planning.

Trade-off: Driver-based forecasts over a black-box model, so finance can explain each change in the plan.

Implementation

  • Data pipelines: pandas or Polars aggregate transaction and customer data into daily and monthly series, with SQL transformations in dbt or DuckDB so every figure can be reproduced. Time zones, currency conversion and corridor mapping are handled explicitly, and the holidays package supplies public holiday calendars for each country.
  • Forecasting models: Prophet models trend and seasonality with holiday effects, statsmodels provides SARIMA and ETS benchmarks, and LightGBM with lag features handles corridor-level series. statsforecast or sktime run rolling-origin backtests, so each model is judged on periods it did not see during fitting.
  • Economics and scenarios: A driver-based layer in pandas applies revenue and cost per transaction to each forecast, with NumPy for sensitivity tables. Base, upside and downside inputs sit in a configuration file, so finance can change assumptions without editing code.
  • KPI breakdown: Usage metrics by segment, channel and corridor are computed with pandas groupby, with variance analysis against forecast using a bridge calculation that explains each difference. Plotly charts show the drivers visually.
  • Reporting: A monthly pack generated with openpyxl and Jinja2, plus a scenario dashboard in Streamlit or Plotly Dash for finance and operations. Scheduled runs use Airflow, Prefect or cron, depending on the platform already in use.

Indicative Roadmap

Phase Timing Deliverable
Data and definitions Weeks 1–2 Agreed KPIs and data sources
Forecast models Weeks 3–5 Backtested activity forecasts by segment
Economics and scenarios Weeks 6–8 Revenue and cost outcomes under three scenarios
Reporting handover Weeks 9–10 Monthly pack and documentation for finance

Measures of success

  • Forecast error is measured monthly against actuals and reported.
  • Revenue and cost outcomes can be traced to activity drivers.
  • The finance team runs the monthly process without support.
